Banana Bread

There's nothing like the smell of banana bread baking in the house! It warms the heart and the aroma is truly wonderful and well received. Banana bread is a comfort food that does not disappoint! It is delicious but did you know that it is best made with bananas that are very ripe? Brown peel bananas are still useful. So, how do you use them to make banana bread?

Well, don't go bananas over trying to make banana bread! The recipe below calls for simple ingredients and is quick to make at home. This no fuss recipe is a treat to all who love eating banana bread. So, let's get started in making banana bread.



Ingredients

  • 1 cup margarine
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/3 cup ripe bananas (use 3 large or 4 small bananas)
  • 1 tsp vanillia extract
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven 350 degrees
  2. Mix butter, sugar, 2 eggs and bananas
  3. Add vanilla extract, flour, baking soda and salt to mixture
  4. Pour mixture into two greased loaf pans
  5. Bake for 50 minutes
