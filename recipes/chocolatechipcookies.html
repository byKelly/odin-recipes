Chocolate Chip Cookies

A classic take on cookies! Chocolate chip cookies are a sure winner with children and adults alike. You can't go wrong in making them. Chocolate chip cookies are good as snacks or as a dessert. They're best served with milk to dunk them in it.

There are several chocolate chip cookie recipes out there. But the recipe below will remind you of the home baked cookies that your grandma made for you when you came to visit her. The cookie recipe is a tried and true favorite that everyone can enjoy! Definitely, these cookies are hands down a crowd pleaser.



Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup butter softened
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven 350 degrees.
  2. Soften the butter in a bowl and then combine it with the brown and white sugar.
  3. Add the egg to the bowl and mix together.
  4. Add the vanilla extract, baking soda and salt.
  5. Add the flour to the mixture and then the chocolate chips.
  6. Use your hands to make balls of cookie dough and place them on a cookie sheet.
  7. Bake for 13 minutes. Then serve cookies with milk.
