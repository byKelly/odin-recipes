Snickerdoodle Cookies

Snickerdoodle cookies are sometimes mistaken as sugar cookies. The key difference? Snickerdoodle cookies have cinnamon that make them taste different. The cookies have a mixture of white sugar and cinnamon.They also have cream of tartar as an ingredient. The cookies are soft and chewy in the middle with crisp edges.

So, if you're in the mood for cookies with a different texture to them, then this recipe is for you. These cookies have a distinct and luscious taste that is unique. They are a wonderful addition as a dessert. See below for this flavorful cookie recipe.



Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter softened
  • 1 1/2 cups of white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
  • 2 3/4 cups of flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons of cream of tartar
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 3/4 teaspoon of salt

Cinnamon-Sugar Mixture

  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons of cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. In a large bowl, cream butter and sugar for 4 to 5 minutes until light and fluffy. Scrape the sides of the bowl and add the eggs and vanilla extract. Cream for 1 to 2 minutes longer.
  3. Stir in flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt, just until combined.
  4. In a small bowl, stir together the sugar and cinnamon.
  5. If time allows, wrap the dough and let refrigerate for 20 to 30 minutes. Roll into small balls until round and smooth. Drop into the cinnamon-sugar mixture and coat well. Using a spoon, coat for a second time, ensuring the cookie balls are completely covered. *To make flatter snickerdoodles, press down in the center of the ball before placing in the oven. This helps to keep them from puffing up in middle.
  6. Place on a parchment paper-lined baking sheet. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes. Let cool for several minutes on baking sheet before removing them from the pan.
